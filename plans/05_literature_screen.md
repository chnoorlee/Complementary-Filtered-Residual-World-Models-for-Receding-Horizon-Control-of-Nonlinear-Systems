# Stage 5 Plan — LITERATURE_SCREEN

## Goal

Reject superficially similar but off-topic retrievals and keep an evidence set that directly informs world-model transitions, hybrid physics, predictive uncertainty, or the evaluation protocol.

## Rules

- Year 2021–2026 inclusive for the core evidence set.
- DOI or official academic URL required.
- Archival versions preferred over duplicate preprints.
- Preprints are labeled and cannot alone establish a mature consensus.
- World-model papers centered on language models, business, medicine, or unrelated vision tasks are excluded.
- A paper is not a baseline unless its prediction mechanism can be instantiated under the shared protocol.

## Novelty audit

The closest method found so far is the 2022 Physics-guided Deep Markov Model (DOI `10.1016/j.ymssp.2022.109276`). It already combines probabilistic latent dynamics with physics, so “physics + uncertainty” is not novel. RGP-WM must instead be evaluated on its narrower claim: **supervised branch-reliability prediction and adaptive expert gating during free rollout under observation/parameter shift**.

## Gate

- >=30 included papers.
- At least four protocol-compatible core comparator anchors.
- No exact match for the full claimed mechanism; otherwise pivot.
