# Stage 6 Plan — KNOWLEDGE_EXTRACT

## Goal

Convert the screened bibliography into evidence cards without adding claims beyond titles, abstracts, archival metadata, and directly inspected source information.

## Focus cards

- recurrent and transformer world-model transition families;
- fixed and probabilistic physics-guided dynamics models;

## Exit criteria

- Every card retains DOI or official URL and cite key.
- Method, evidence, and limitation fields are separated.
- Preprint status is explicit.
