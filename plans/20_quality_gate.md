# Stage 20 — Quality Gate

## Pass conditions

- >=7/10 on novelty, rigor, evidence, clarity, and reproducibility; no axis below 6.
- 29+ tests pass; full result counts and hashes present.
- No pending result macro, undefined citation/reference, NaN/Inf, or unsupported SOTA/acceptance claim.
- All hypotheses have explicit supported/not-supported decisions.

Failure routes to Stage 13 or 19.
