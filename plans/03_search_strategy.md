# Stage 3 Plan — SEARCH_STRATEGY

## Goal

Build a reproducible literature search for 2021-01-01 through 2026-08-05 that identifies protocol-relevant world-model, hybrid-physics, uncertainty, and evaluation work.

## Sources

- OpenAlex API: discovery and metadata.
- DBLP API: archival computer-science venue verification.
- Crossref API: DOI metadata verification.
- arXiv API/official paper pages: preprints without archival versions.
- Publisher and conference proceedings pages: final spot checks for core claims.

## Gate

At least 30 retained academic records; at least 90% of core citations in the date window; all core comparators dated 2021 or later; DOI or official URL for every retained record.
