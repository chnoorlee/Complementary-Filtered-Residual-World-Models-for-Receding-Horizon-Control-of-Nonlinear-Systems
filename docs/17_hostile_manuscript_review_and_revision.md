# Hostile manuscript review and revision record

Date: 2026-10-20

## Initial decision

The independent hostile review assigned 6/10 and recommended major revision.
Its central concern was not the locked arithmetic or disclosure of the failed
H2 gate. It was the gap between a controlled synthetic transition-prediction
study and broader language about corruption-tolerant world models.

## Findings and completed revisions

1. **External-validity boundary.** The title was narrowed to *A Confirmatory
   Study of Complementary-Filtered Residual World Models under Synthetic
   Context Corruption*. The abstract, discussion, limitations, and conclusion
   now restrict the evidence to five low-dimensional analytic systems with
   known future controls. No real-robot, visual-control, policy-quality, or
   universal-filter claim is made.
2. **Mechanism identification.** The paper now states that the bundled
   ID-corrupt and OOD-corrupt regimes do not separate observation noise,
   missingness, and parameter shift. The observed gains therefore do not
   causally identify denoising, imputation, or shift handling as the mechanism.
3. **Comparator identity.** Comparator names are consistently qualified as
   compact, protocol-matched, family-inspired implementations. They are not
   presented as exact reproductions of the cited systems or as a
   state-of-the-art leaderboard comparison.
4. **Paper-alone detail.** The appendix now reports the nominal and shifted
   parameter intervals, compact architecture specifications, gain behavior,
   the six-trial tuning budget, and the selected trial index for every
   system--method pair.
5. **Inference scope.** The manuscript preserves the fixed eight-seed
   estimand, exact sign-flip test, percentile bootstrap, and symmetry
   assumption. It does not generalize these results to an open population of
   physical systems.

## Resolution

The revised manuscript supports a bounded confirmatory claim on the frozen
synthetic benchmark. That claim does not require a new experiment. A broader
claim about corruption mechanisms, structurally misspecified physics, real
measurements, embodied control, or robot transfer would require new,
preregistered experiments and is outside this package.

This revision record is not evidence of journal acceptance or current
quartile status.
