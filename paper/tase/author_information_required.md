# Author-controlled information required before T-ASE submission

The anonymous review package is intentionally separate from the following
information. Complete these fields in the submission system and any required
nonanonymous title-page or cover-letter file:

- author names and order;
- affiliations, cities, and countries;
- corresponding-author email and ORCID;
- funding source and grant number, or an explicit no-funding statement;
- competing-interests statement;
- author contributions;
- any required data/code archive DOI and the date on which it becomes public;
- final confirmation that every author approves the submitted files;
- T-ASE methodology and application classification codes selected in the
  submission system.

Candidate classifications based on the anonymous manuscript are AI/machine
learning, modeling and simulation, system identification/diagnosis, and control
theory under methodology; autonomous systems, aerospace, or robotics under
application. The submitting authors must choose the codes shown by the current
T-ASE submission form.
