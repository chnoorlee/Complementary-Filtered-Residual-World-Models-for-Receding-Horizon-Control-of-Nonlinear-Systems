\section{Limitations}

The controlled evidence covers five equation families, eight independent
data-training seed clusters, one deterministic-mean cross-entropy controller,
a 20-step regulation interval, and a quadratic objective. The component
analyses reuse fitted checkpoints. The structured missingness test is a
synchronous two-step all-channel outage inside an eight-step context; longer
bursts, informative dropout, delayed measurements, sensor-specific failures,
and actuator faults are not represented.

The measured-flight evidence contains three held-out trajectories from one
platform and collection session. Targets are the dataset authors' offline-
preprocessed state estimates, and forecasts use recorded future motor inputs.
These records test offline prediction, not online intervention. Physical
closed-loop control, raw-sensor estimation, cross-platform transfer, task
constraints, and controller timing on embedded hardware remain unevaluated.

The flight prior omits angular acceleration, drag, and motor lag. A uniform
fixed gain cannot track channel- or time-dependent reliability. The diagonal-
Gaussian output omits cross-state covariance and multimodality, and its
50-step marginal intervals under-cover on the held-out flights. Unit-circle
projection couples pendulum coordinates after the mask-gated correction. No
stability certificate follows from the bounded residual or the finite-horizon
open- and closed-loop results.
