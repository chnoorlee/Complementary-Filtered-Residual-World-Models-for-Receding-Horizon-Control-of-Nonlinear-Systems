\opening{Dear Editor-in-Chief,}

Please consider the manuscript entitled ``Complementary-Filtered Residual
World Models for Receding-Horizon Control of Nonlinear Systems'' for
publication in the \emph{International Journal of Control, Automation, and
Systems}.

The manuscript studies a state-estimation and predictive-control problem in
which forecast-origin observation error and transition error accumulate over a
receding horizon. It introduces a complementary-filtered residual world model
that combines a mask-gated context update, nominal dynamics, and a bounded
neural residual. The evaluation comprises five nonlinear controlled systems,
a frozen 1,920-episode receding-horizon control study, and held-out measured
NanoDrone flight records.

The results distinguish the supported contribution from the components that
do not receive additional empirical support. The complete model improves on
the preselected physics-guided neural comparator in the aggregate controlled
benchmark and in realized closed-loop state loss. At the same time,
clean-regime noninferiority is not established, filtering followed by nominal
dynamics slightly outperforms the complete model, and the measured-flight
study exposes higher full-state error and undercoverage despite lower
long-horizon position error. These findings are relevant to the journal's
scope in control engineering, automation, and artificial intelligence for
engineered systems because they identify the practical value of
forecast-origin filtering while delimiting the contribution of the learned
residual and uncertainty output.

The manuscript contains the author information, data and code availability
statement, funding statement, competing-interest declaration, author
contribution statement, and applicable ethics and consent statements. Complete
editable LaTeX sources and figure files accompany the submission.

Thank you for considering this manuscript.

\closing{Sincerely,}
